Can AI spend your money without you knowing? Not quite—but the checkout button is getting closer.

Imagine asking an AI: find me the best laptop under one thousand dollars. It can search, compare specifications, check sellers, and narrow the choices.

But selecting is not the same as paying. At checkout, emerging systems from Visa and Mastercard are designed around consumer-approved instructions, tokenized payment credentials, and registered or validated agents.

You can set the product, merchant, price ceiling, quantity, and time window. Depending on the flow, you may still confirm the purchase and authenticate with a passkey, biometrics, or another approved method.

The payment network and issuer can then evaluate the request against those controls and existing fraud checks.

These systems are emerging. Not every assistant can buy things, and no safeguard is perfect.

So, would you trust an AI with a one-time budget—or never let it reach checkout?
