## Accuracy boundaries used in the video

- AI product selection is not itself a payment authorization.
- Agentic payment programs are emerging and require participating agents, wallets, merchants, issuers, and payment-network controls.
- A user-authorized instruction can specify boundaries such as product, merchant, price ceiling, quantity, or validity window, depending on the implementation.
- Authentication may use passkeys, biometrics, OTP, or another approved method; the exact experience varies.
- Agent registration/signatures help identify a trusted participant; they do not make fraud or implementation failure impossible.
- Existing payment-network and issuer checks still participate in authorization.
